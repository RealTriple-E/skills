# Example — SaaS Company

**Objective:** Awareness + conversion for a new project-management feature.  
**Audience:** Project managers and team leads in mid-size companies.  
**Autonomous mode:** Approval (human confirms before publish).  
**Execution:** browser-session-persistent.

## Approach

1. Objective Engine ranks Awareness primary, conversion secondary.  
2. Audience model emphasizes pain of tool-switching and visibility gaps.  
3. Content-Market Fit: “See the work that is slipping through the cracks” angle.  
4. Format: short demonstration insight + clear value, not feature dump.  
5. Voice DNA: professional, direct, moderate technical depth, low emoji.  
6. Brand Guardrails: no absolute “guaranteed productivity” claims.  
7. Quality gates pass → Scheduled on Kanban with target time.  
8. Publisher loads persistent session only after human approval.  
9. Analyst later attaches reply themes and profile-visit lift.

## Sample Pillar Mix for Launch Window

- 40% Educational (how the problem shows up)  
- 25% Product demonstration insights  
- 20% Customer/problem stories  
- 15% Conversation starters
