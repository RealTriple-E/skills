# Example — Technology / Infrastructure Account

**Objective:** Thought leadership and developer trust.  
**Audience:** Senior engineers, architects, technical founders.  
**Autonomous mode:** Assisted or Approval.  
**Execution:** browser-session-persistent.

## Approach

1. High technical depth Voice DNA.  
2. Content-Market Fit prioritizes original analysis over news amplification.  
3. Algorithm guidance: meaningful replies and dwell matter more than raw like count.  
4. Competitive Intelligence looks for implementation gaps competitors ignore.  
5. Strict “Do Not Post” on saturated hype cycles where the account has nothing new to add.
