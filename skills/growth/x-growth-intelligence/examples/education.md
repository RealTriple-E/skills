# Example — Educational / University Account

**Objective:** Authority + education + community among students and practitioners.  
**Audience:** Students, early-career professionals, educators in the subject domain.  
**Autonomous mode:** Approval.  
**Execution:** browser-session-persistent.

## Approach

1. Heavy Educational pillar.  
2. Audience model distinguishes beginner vs intermediate knowledge levels; write accordingly.  
3. Threads used for deeper explanations; single posts for sharp insights or questions.  
4. Voice DNA: clear, moderately formal, high teaching intent, low provocation.  
5. Accessibility: prefer plain language, encourage alt text for any media.  
6. Geographic Intelligence respects academic calendars and local term timings.
