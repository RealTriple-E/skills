# Human Writing Engine + Voice DNA

Author: Elyazer Emmanuel

## Goal

Produce posts that sound like a thoughtful human who knows the account, not like generic AI social content.

## Patterns to Detect and Remove

- “In today’s fast-paced world…”
- Excessive em dashes
- Predictable three-part structures
- Artificial controversy or manufactured outrage
- Repetitive sentence lengths
- Generic motivational language
- Unnecessary headings inside short posts
- Excessive or decorative emojis
- “Here’s the thing:”
- “Let that sink in.”
- “The future is…”
- Generic “10 lessons” / “5 ways” listicles without original insight
- Overly polished corporate speak when the Voice DNA is direct
- Filler transitions that add no information

## Voice DNA (per Account Profile)

Capture and apply:

```
Formality:        x/10
Technical depth:  x/10
Humor:            x/10
Directness:       x/10
Provocation:      x/10
Storytelling:     x/10
Emoji usage:      x/10
Sentence length:  short / medium / long / mixed
Preferred vocabulary
Avoid list
```

Two accounts using the same skill must be able to sound completely different.

## Process

1. Load Voice DNA from Account Profile (or infer carefully from provided examples).
2. Draft for the active Objective + Audience + Content-Market Fit.
3. Run a self-critique pass against the “Patterns to Remove” list.
4. Adjust tone, length, and vocabulary to match Voice DNA.
5. Prefer concrete observations, original perspective, and useful detail over abstract claims.
6. For threads: each post should stand alone usefully while advancing the whole.

## Brand Guardrails Integration

After voice pass, enforce Brand Guardrails (terminology, claims, pricing, legal language, approved phrases). See `references/brand-safety.md`.

## Output

Final draft + brief rationale of why the chosen angle, format and voice serve the objective and audience.
