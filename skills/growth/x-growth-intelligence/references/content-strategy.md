# Content Strategy Engines

Author: Elyazer Emmanuel

## Objective Engine

Never assume “viral posts”. Determine the active objective first. Strategy, format, tone and success metrics all change with the objective.

Common objectives:

- Awareness  
- Authority / Thought leadership  
- Engagement  
- Community  
- Leads  
- Sales  
- Traffic  
- App adoption  
- Recruitment  
- Education  
- Customer retention  
- Launch  
- Crisis communication  
- Reputation  

Multiple objectives may be active; rank them.

## Content-Pillar Engine

Construct a balanced mix appropriate to the Account Profile and objectives. Example starting distribution (adjust from performance data):

- Educational  
- Opinion / Perspective  
- Industry insights  
- Community / conversation  
- Product / offer  
- Experimental  

Percentages are not fixed. Learn the effective distribution over time from account data.

## Content-Market Fit Engine

Before writing, evaluate:

Topic × Audience × Timing × Perspective × Value

Ask: “Why would somebody care about this right now?”  
A technically correct post can still be a poor social post. Identify the opportunity, not merely generate prose.

## Trend Detection + Relevance Filter

Detect: breaking news, emerging conversations, trending subjects, industry discussions, recurring debates, new products, policy changes, viral narratives, competitor activity, audience questions, underserved topics.

Classify each opportunity:

- NOW — publish quickly  
- TODAY — relevant but not urgent  
- THIS WEEK — evergreen/trending opportunity  
- EVERGREEN — can be published anytime  
- AVOID — low-value, saturated, or risky  

**Mandatory filter (never skip):**

```
Is it relevant to the audience?
Is it relevant to the account?
Can we add original value?
Can we say something credible?
Would posting strengthen the brand?
→ YES = create   NO = ignore
```

## Recommendation Eligibility

See `references/x-algorithm.md`. Shift mindset from “will this go viral?” to “does this have qualities that make it worth recommending?”

## Campaign Mode

Multi-phase sequences (e.g. Product launch):

Phase 1 Awareness → Phase 2 Education → Phase 3 Proof → Phase 4 Launch → Phase 5 Conversion → Phase 6 Follow-up

Link phases on the Kanban board so later phases depend on earlier ones reaching Published.

## Funnel Awareness

Discovery → Interest → Education → Trust → Consideration → Action → Retention

Map individual posts and campaigns onto the funnel stage they serve.

## “Do Not Post” Intelligence

Explicitly recommend against posting when:

- Insufficient evidence  
- Irrelevant or saturated trend  
- Reputational or brand risk  
- Weak hook  
- Duplicate of recent content  
- Poor timing  
- Unclear objective  
- Potential misinformation  
- Likely negative audience reaction  

Record the reason (especially on Kanban via block + comment).
