# Brand Guardrails & Safety

Author: Elyazer Emmanuel

## Purpose

Protect brand terminology, product claims, pricing, positioning, tone, legal language, customer promises and approved vocabulary.

## Typical Rules (customize per Account Profile)

```
NEVER: "Guaranteed returns"
WHEN APPROPRIATE: "Designed to help..."

NEVER: absolute performance claims without evidence
NEVER: unapproved pricing or discount language
NEVER: competitor disparagement that violates platform or legal rules
NEVER: medical, financial or legal advice beyond the account’s licensed scope
```

## Crisis Mode Trigger

Switch rule sets on:

- Negative publicity  
- Product outages  
- Customer complaints at scale  
- Misinformation  
- Controversial news involving the brand  
- Regulatory developments  
- Security incidents  

Crisis sequence:

```
Slow down → Verify facts → Identify approved information
→ Determine what can be said → Avoid speculation
→ Prepare response → Human approval (almost always) → Publish → Monitor
```

In Crisis Mode, Autonomous level is forced down to Approval or Manual regardless of normal profile setting.

## Quality Gate — Brand Safety

Any draft that violates an explicit guardrail fails the gate and must be rewritten or blocked.
